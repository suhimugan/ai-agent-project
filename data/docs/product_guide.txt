Product Setup
Download the installer from our website and run it as administrator.
The setup wizard will guide you through the installation in under 5 minutes.
Supported platforms: Windows 10/11, macOS 12+, Ubuntu 20.04+.

Troubleshooting
If the app fails to launch, restart your computer and try again.
For persistent issues, delete the cache folder at AppData/Local/OurApp/cache.
Contact support with your error log if the problem continues.
