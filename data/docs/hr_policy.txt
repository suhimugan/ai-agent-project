Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
Sick leave is separate and capped at 10 days per year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance.
Unused annual leave can be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 10 days.

Onboarding
New employees must complete onboarding within their first 2 weeks.
This includes IT setup, HR documentation, and a team introduction session.

Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted every 6 months.
Employees receive written feedback and a rating from 1 to 5.
Ratings below 2 trigger a performance improvement plan.
